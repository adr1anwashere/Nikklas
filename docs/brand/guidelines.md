# Nikklas brand guidelines v1.1

The designed version of this book is `Nikklas_Brand_Guidelines_v1.1.pdf`. Details live in the sibling
files: `identity.md` (logo, colour, type, accessibility), `voice.md`, `messaging.md`, `positioning.md`.
Tokens for the app: `tokens.css`.

## About this guide
For the four people building Nikklas and anyone who makes something with its name on it: app screens,
the proposal, the pitch deck, pilot invites and social posts. Read Part 1 once; keep Parts 2 to 5 open
while you work.

## Part 1. Foundation
- **Mission:** help every student find study people for the next exam without broadcasting themselves to the whole class.
- **Vision:** in every large course, no one prepares for UTS alone because they did not know who to ask.
- **Values:** privacy first (nickname, major and year only) · small groups (2 to 8 seats) · low pressure (invite, never push) · fair seats (first tap wins) · clear words (one idea per line).
- **Personality:** warm, low-pressure, practical, local, tidy.
- **Positioning:** for Indonesian university students who want a study group but will not ask in the class chat, Nikklas is the campus study-group finder that matches you by course, class section and exam period, because sessions hold 2 to 8 seats, rooms are members-only and no one sees your phone number.

## Part 2. Visual identity
Logo, colour, type, imagery and icons: `identity.md` sections 02 to 06.

## Part 3. Verbal identity
Voice essence: *a classmate who saved you a seat: warm, brief and practical.* Rules: `voice.md`.
The three rules that matter most: use "kamu", write in sentence case, and say exactly what is shared.

## Part 4. Messaging
Core message, tagline ("Temukan teman belajarmu. / Find your study people.") and key messages: `messaging.md`.

## Part 5. Brand in use
- **App:** `tokens.css`; one Clay button per screen; grid paper ground behind, white cards in front.
- **Proposal and reports:** A4, Fraunces headings at 400, Figtree 10 pt body, hairline tables, Clay only for section numbers and links. Tagged PDF with title, language and bookmarks.
- **Pitch deck:** one idea per slide; headline in Fraunces 400, 40 pt or more; real app screens, not mock-ups drawn from scratch; numbers only from the pilot or the code.
- **App icon and avatars:** `nikklas-mark-reversed.svg` (white mark on Clay), never the full lockup.
- **Pilot invites and social:** kamu-form, one ask, one fact. No countdowns.

## Brand don'ts
- [ ] Never write the name "Nikllas" or "NIKKLAS"; it is Nikklas.
- [ ] Never use the logo under 16 px (mark) or 96 px (lockup).
- [ ] Never use a second accent colour, violet or lavender.
- [ ] Never put body text on the grid paper; put it on a white card.
- [ ] Never use Inter, emoji icons or Fraunces for body text.
- [ ] Never show status by colour alone.
- [ ] Never use "Anda" in Indonesian copy.
- [ ] Never claim verified students, AI matching or user numbers the pilot has not measured.
