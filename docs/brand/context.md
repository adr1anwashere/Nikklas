github.com/adr1anwashere/Nikklas (commit 8f8cfaf, 21 Nov 2026), and the semifinal proposal PDF.

## Brand
- **Name**: Nikklas (two k, one l). The team is called Satu Orang Satu Ubin; the team name is not part of the product brand.
- **Category**: campus study-group finder, mobile-first web app
- **Description**: Indonesian university students find or post a study session for a shared course, class section and exam period, join with one tap, chat in a private room and agree on a meeting place. Phone numbers are never shown.
- **Stage**: pre-launch (core MVP done; STACK 2026 semifinal; pilot planned 1 to 15 Dec 2026)
- **Website**: none yet (repo only)

## Audience
- **Primary audience**: first- and second-year undergraduates in large shared courses (100 to 300+ students), in the weeks before Kuis, UTS and UAS.
- **High-need segments**: newcomers and transfer students without a class network; commuters and *perantau* who leave campus right after class; shy students who will not post in a 300-person chat.
- **Key problem**: there is no structured, low-pressure way to find classmates who want to study the same chapter for the same exam. Class chats bury the request and expose phone numbers.
- **Their language** (from the repo's docs and demo script): "belajar bareng", "Ada yang mau belajar bareng?", "kelompok belajar", "teman belajar", "kelas K01", "latihan soal UTS tahun lalu", "kosan", "mahasiswa perantau", "komuter".

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: filters that match how Indonesian courses work (course code, class section, exam period, topic); a hard seat limit of 2 to 8 enforced in the database; nickname, major and year only; a meeting place card with a map link.
- **Competitors**: WhatsApp and LINE class groups; campus LMS forums; Instagram, X and Discord servers.
- **Market position**: niche, free campus tool, one faculty at a time.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality words**: warm, low-pressure, practical, local, tidy
- **Tone**: casual and friendly, never shaming; short sentences; Bahasa Indonesia first
- **Voice admires**: none named by the team

## Values & Mission
- **Core values**: privacy first; small groups; low pressure; fair seats (first tap wins); clear words
- **Mission**: help every student find study people for the next exam without broadcasting themselves to the whole class.

## Goals
- **Primary goal**: a successful invite-only pilot with 7 students in one faculty, then the STACK 2026 final (16 Dec 2026).
- **Key metrics**: median time to fill under 4 hours; 60% or more of sessions fill; comfort rating 4.0 of 5 or more.
