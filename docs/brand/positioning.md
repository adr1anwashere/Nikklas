# Positioning

## 01 Category
- **Stated category**: study-group finder.
- **Actual category**: what students use today to find study partners, which is the class WhatsApp or LINE group.
- **Recommendation**: position against the class group chat, not against other apps. Students do not compare Nikklas with an app store category; they compare it with typing "Ada yang mau belajar bareng?" into a 300-person chat.

## 02 Map
Axis 1: public broadcast ↔ private small group. Axis 2: unstructured chat ↔ structured by course and exam.

- **WhatsApp / LINE class groups**: public and unstructured. Everyone is there, nothing is filtered, phone numbers are visible.
- **Campus LMS forums**: structured by course, but public and formal; the student ID shows.
- **Instagram, X, Discord**: public and unstructured; personal accounts show.
- **Nikklas**: private and structured. Course, section, exam period and topic decide who you meet; a room only opens for members.

The private and structured corner is empty.

## 03 Territory
- **The space**: the small, private study group for one exam.
- **The audience owned**: the student who would study with classmates but will not ask in the class chat.
- **The moat**: database rules that the class chat cannot copy (members-only rooms, a hard seat limit, nickname-only profiles) plus filters shaped around Indonesian course codes, class sections and Kuis/UTS/UAS.

## 04 Statement
For Indonesian university students who want a study group but will not ask in the class chat,
Nikklas is the campus study-group finder that matches you by course, class section and exam period,
because sessions hold 2 to 8 seats, rooms are members-only and no one sees your phone number.

- **Internal**: Nikklas owns the private, exam-shaped small group that class chats and LMS forums cannot offer.
- **Public**: Find classmates studying the same chapter for the same exam, join a small group in one tap, and keep your number to yourself.

## 05 Proof points
1. Filters for course, class section, exam period and topic, run in the database query.
2. Seats are claimed through one database function that locks the session row; two taps on the last seat give one winner.
3. Row Level Security on all 6 tables; chats and meeting places are members-only.
4. Profiles hold nickname, major, year and up to 5 topics. No phone number field exists.
5. Bahasa Indonesia and English on every screen.

## 06 What Nikklas refuses to be
- Not a social network: no friend lists, followers or ratings.
- Not a matching algorithm: students choose the session themselves.
- Not a video-call tool: the group meets in person; the app gets them to the table.
- Never a place where a phone number is required.

## 07 In one sentence
Nikklas finds you a small, private study group for your exact course, class and exam, without sharing your phone number.
